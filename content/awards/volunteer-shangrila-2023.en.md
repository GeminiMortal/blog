## How It Started

In August 2023, I joined a volunteer team in Shangri-La, Yunnan, participating in the Teach for Rural China rural education support project, bringing a series of enrichment classes to children at local schools.

## Daily Volunteering

The service totalled 50 hours, with classes covering four directions:

1. **World citizenship** — opening children's eyes to the world beyond the map
2. **Art** — crafts and painting, so expression goes beyond words
3. **Business & entrepreneurship** — using small simulation games to teach the basics of "value exchange"
4. **Innovative thinking** — open-ended topics that encouraged them to propose their own solutions

> Explaining what you know to someone else is the most rigorous test of understanding. The late nights writing lesson plans forced me to think every concept through more deeply.

## Outcome

This experience later became the practical component of the ASDAN Volunteering Short Course certification — 50 service hours were fully documented, and the course design and delivery were submitted as supporting evidence.
