## About CSP-S

CSP-S is CCF's non-professional software capability certification for the Advanced group. Compared to the Entry level (CSP-J), it steps up in both algorithmic depth and modelling complexity — graph theory, dynamic programming and greedy strategies are unavoidable topics.

## Preparation

Prepared alongside CSP-J in the same year. Round 1 of the Advanced level has more problems and tighter time limits. Beyond drilling problems, the crucial skill is learning to allocate effort in the exam hall: secure the problems you can do first, then return to tackle the harder ones.

> What the Advanced level taught me was not a specific algorithm, but how to break down and approach a completely unfamiliar problem.

Achieving Third Prize in both Entry and Advanced levels marked the end of my informatics competition chapter and the beginning of my engineering practice at university.
