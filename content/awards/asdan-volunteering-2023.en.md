## About the ASDAN Short Course

The ASDAN Volunteering Short Course is a volunteering module within the UK ASDAN quality education certification system. It requires participants to complete a set number of service hours and submit a portfolio of supporting evidence — planning, records and reflection are all essential.

## My Certification Path

The August 2023 Shangri-La teaching trip (Teach for Rural China, 50 hours) formed the practical core of this certification. I systematically compiled all materials from the volunteering period:

1. **Service plan** — course design documents and teaching objectives
2. **Process records** — daily lesson content and student feedback
3. **Reflection summary** — thoughts on the two-way relationship between "teaching" and "learning"

Having met all requirements upon review, I was awarded 6 ASDAN credits.

> The value of volunteering is never a one-way act of giving — the process of re-learning something in order to explain it clearly is where the most solid growth happens.
