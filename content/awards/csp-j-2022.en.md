## About CSP-J

CSP-J is CCF's non-professional software capability certification for beginners. Round 1 is a written test covering computer fundamentals and algorithmic ability, examining data structures, algorithm design and program logic.

## Preparation

During my 2022 preparation, I trained systematically toward a single goal for the first time: working through past papers, cataloguing mistakes, and reviewing every problem I couldn't solve. Third Prize in the Entry group isn't a dazzling result, but it confirmed my interest in "solving problems with code."

> The value of a certification lies not in the certificate itself, but in how it forces you to distinguish between "writing code" and "solving problems."

That same year I also took the Advanced level (CSP-S). Together, the two results became the starting point for choosing Software Engineering as my major.
