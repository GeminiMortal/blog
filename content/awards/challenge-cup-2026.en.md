## Project Background

"Light Guard" addresses the safety pain points of approximately 300 million electric two-wheeler users: poor night visibility, large turning blind spots, and no evidence to rely on after an accident. As a retrofit intelligent driver-assistance system, it does not depend on vehicle manufacturers — ordinary users can simply install it to gain active safety capabilities.

## My Role in the Team

As a core team member, I participated in system solution design and technical roadmap evaluation. I was responsible for integrating the three capabilities of perception, alerting and recording into a complete product story, and delivered the pitch at the provincial defense.

## System Capabilities

1. **Real-time traffic perception** — cameras with dynamic calibration for distance estimation, identifying vehicles and obstacles ahead
2. **Multi-level risk alerts** — graduated audio/visual and vibration warnings covering different urgency levels
3. **Event Data Recording (EDR)** — automatic evidence capture of collision and hard-braking events, providing data to support liability determination
