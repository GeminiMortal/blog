Looking back, every node on this road is quite accidental, and it is quite inevitable to string together.

## Starting point: CSP

In 2022, I participated in CCF's CSP-JS certification, and got a third grade at the entry level and an improved level. The scores were not dazzling, but that was the first time I systematically trained algorithms and programming for a goal-brushing questions, reviewing questions, and controlling my mentality in the examination room.

> The significance of certification is not the certificate itself, but that it forces you to distinguish between "being able to write code" and "being able to solve problems."

## Midfield: A teaching session

In August 2023, I went to Shangri-La to do a teaching session for 50 hours, teaching local children about world citizenship, art, business and entrepreneurship. When I wrote the lesson plan in the early morning, I realized: ** Telling others what you know is the toughest test of understanding **.

That trip also earned 6 credits from the ASDAN Volunteer Short Course.

## Now: Turn ideas into products

After entering the software engineering of Shenyang University of Technology, the rhythm changed-it was no longer just brushing the questions, but doing complete things:

1. **MacIsland**: macOS Smart Island application, a macOS native implementation of Pyisland open source ecosystem. SwiftUI writing animation, AppKit tube window, community wallpaper GitHub OAuth. Now v2.5.1.
2. ** Qingqi Guard **: Rear mounted driving assistance system for two-wheeled vehicles-camera + dynamic customized distance estimation, multi-level sound and light/vibration alarms, and event recording (EDR) to leave evidence for accident responsibility determination. This project won the third prize in the 2026 "Challenge Cup" provincial competition.

## A little feeling

- ** Participating in competitions is a means, not an end **: CSP taught me to solve problems, and Challenge Cup taught me to explain problems clearly
- ** Records are valuable **: So with this blog
- ** Ecology is more interesting than a single point **: A smart island, there are three implementations of Python/Electron/Tauri on Windows, and I make up for macOS-collaboration is much more fun than fighting alone

Next step: Polish what you are doing better, and then keep writing.
